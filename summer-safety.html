Summer Safety

Summer in Moreno Valley can bring dangerous heat and poor air quality. Knowing where to cool off and taking a few simple precautions can help protect you, your family, and your pets.

⚠️ HEAT SAFETY NOTICE

Dangerous heat and poor air quality can increase the risk of heat-related illness. Take extra precautions to protect yourself, your family, and your pets.

🌡️ Extreme Heat Safety

Hot weather can become dangerous quickly. Drink plenty of water, avoid strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest hours, wear lightweight clothing, and never leave children or pets in a parked vehicle.

🌬️ Air Quality

Smoke, ozone, and other pollutants can make the air unhealthy to breathe. If air quality is poor, limit prolonged outdoor activity and monitor current conditions before exercising or working outside.

🐾 Pet Safety

High temperatures can be dangerous for pets, too. Provide plenty of fresh water and shade, avoid hot pavement, and never leave pets unattended inside a vehicle.
