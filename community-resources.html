TRANSPORTATION HELP FOR JOB INTERVIEWS

Need a ride to a job interview?

Free transportation options are available in Moreno Valley and Riverside County—but you have to request them.

Call 211 (Fastest Option)

Dial 211 or visit:
211 Riverside County

Ask for:

“Help getting to job interviews or job training”

You may receive:

  • Free Lyft rides
  • Help getting to job interviews or training

Notes:

  • Eligibility required
  • Typically limited rides (ex: one round trip)
  • Must be for an essential need (job interviews qualify)

Lyft Jobs Access Program

Learn More About Lyft Jobs Access

Provides free or discounted rides for:

  • Job interviews
  • First weeks of employment
  • Job training programs

Important:

  • Not available directly in the app
  • Must go through a partner (workforce programs, nonprofits, or 211 referral)

Local Workforce Programs

Organizations in Riverside County may provide transportation help along with job placement services.

Contact:

  • Riverside County Workforce Development
  • Goodwill (Inland Empire)

May include:

  • Lyft ride credits
  • Bus passes
  • Employment support

Backup Option: Bus Pass Assistance

Riverside County Transportation Services

Some programs offer:

  • Free or discounted bus passes for job-related travel

Important

  • There is no app that automatically provides free rides
  • Most programs require eligibility or referral
  • Request assistance before your interview date

Tip

If you need help, say:

“I don’t have reliable transportation and need a ride to a job interview.”

This helps route you to available programs faster.
